About Our School

Nurturing happy, confident and responsible young learners at Kumaratchi since 2013.

Our Philosophy

Building a strong foundation in every child

The educational philosophy at Thirugnanasambandar School focuses on nurturing young minds through a joyful, caring, and engaging learning environment. Our aim is to build a strong foundation in every child by developing essential academic skills, creativity, confidence, good values, and curiosity.

From LKG to Grade 5, we encourage children to explore, discover, learn, and grow through interactive teaching methods and meaningful activities. We strive to shape happy, confident, and responsible young learners who are ready to embrace future opportunities.

We achieve this through a thoughtfully designed curriculum that includes:

  • Strong reading, writing & numeracy
  • Spoken English every day
  • Art, craft & creativity
  • Abacus & competitions
  • Prayers, values & culture
  • Eco & nature activities
  • Festivals & celebrations
  • Regular unit assessments
Our History

Founded in remembrance, built to serve

The school started in the year 2013 in remembrance of late Thirugnanasambandam, son of Nattar Pakrisamy from Keelaparuthikudi village, to serve the local people here.

It is managed by Amsayal Educational Trust and is situated on Chidambaram Main Road, Kumaratchi – 608 302, in Kattumannar Koil Taluk, Cuddalore District. The school is registered with the Government (Reg. No. 03/195/2014).

Aims & Objectives

More than toppers — fine human beings

The trust of the school is not merely to create toppers in the Board Exams, but to form fine human beings deeply rooted in the true cultural values of our motherland, upholding its heritage of unity in diversity — making each child a patriot and a world citizen.

Work Together

To reach out to one and all and work with people of good will for Truth, Love, Justice, Equality, National Integration and Communal Harmony.

Integrated Growth

To help children grow into integrated human beings through spiritual, moral, physical, intellectual and emotional development.

Core Values

To inculcate love, acceptance, forgiveness, justice, sharing and respect for all, and to build a healthy environment.

Unity in Diversity

To respect all religions and cultures in our country and strive to achieve “Unity in Diversity”.

Community Ties

To work together for common goals with institutions and organisations in the neighbourhood.

A New Society

To give all staff, students, parents and well-wishers the experience of a new society in this millennium.
