Rules, Policy & Parents Corner

Guidelines from the school handbook that help every child learn in a safe, orderly environment.

School Policy

What we expect from every pupil

  1. Strict regularity, obedience, courtesy in speech and conduct, and cleanliness of dress and person are expected from every pupil.
  2. Wearing the school uniform is obligatory. No student will be allowed on the premises without the prescribed uniform.
  3. School property must be cared for. Any damage, even by accident, must be reported at once; wilful damage will be made good by the student responsible.
  4. The date of birth of a student, once entered, shall not be changed.
  5. English is the medium of instruction, so children should speak only in English on the school premises.
  6. Late-comers and absentees will not be admitted to class without the Principal's permission.
  7. Students may not leave the school premises without the Principal's permission.
  8. No leave of absence is granted except on written application from parents.
  9. Every student is expected to be on time daily for the assembly.
Rules of the School

Good to know

  1. Pupils should be present at school by 9.15 a.m.
  2. Pupils are expected to keep the classrooms and the school premises absolutely clean.
  3. Pupils should not come to school wearing costly jewels; the management is not responsible if they are lost.
  4. Pupils should not be absent without prior information.
  5. Parents are requested to attend parent–teacher meetings without fail.
  6. Parents should check progress reports and meet the Class Teacher and/or Principal if they are not satisfied with their child's progress.
To Parents — Notification

Parents corner

Please read these carefully. Parents must go through the school diary every day.

Attendance

Students must attend class regularly. Pupils with less than 75% attendance will not be allowed to write the examination. Absence on re-opening day is not allowed.

Leave & Health

Leave exceeding a week must be supported by a medical certificate. Students who are sick will not be permitted to attend class.

Examinations

A test is held at the end of every unit. Missing exams without genuine reasons will not be entertained. Students must attend special classes.

Uniform & Hygiene

Students should always be clean and well dressed in complete school uniform; otherwise they are liable to be sent home.

English Campus

All pupils should talk only in English on campus and in class with their teachers and other pupils.

Money & Lunch

No money should be brought to school by pupils. During the lunch break pupils are requested to leave their classrooms.

Term fees should be paid in the months of June, September and January every year. See the Admissions page for bank details.

Give your child a joyful start.

Admissions are open from LKG to Grade 5. Visit our campus at Kumaratchi.
